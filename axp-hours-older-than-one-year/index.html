What happens to AXP hours older than one year?

They still count, at 75%. Since November 18, 2025, experience you report within one year of earning it gets 100% credit. Anything older gets 75%, with no age limit.

An example

You worked 1,400 hours in Project Development & Documentation and report them 14 months later. NCARB credits 1,050. The other 350 hours, about two months of full-time work, are gone for licensure purposes.

What if your hours sit on both sides of the one-year line?

Report them separately. NCARB lets one report cover up to 12 months back from when you last earned experience, and that report earns 100%. Experience older than one year goes in its own report, with no limit on the time frame, and earns 75%.

How to avoid it

Report at least every few months. A report can reach back 12 months, but anything you leave longer than a year drops to 75%, so waiting puts your oldest months at risk.
