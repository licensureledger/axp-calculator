How the one-year rule works

NCARB changed AXP reporting on November 18, 2025. Experience reported within one year of when you earned it counts at 100%. Anything older still counts, at 75%, with no age limit. So 100 hours reported late becomes 75 credited hours. The fix is simple: report at least every few months.

Required hours by area

The six areas and their minimums total 3,740 hours. Hours above an area's minimum don't carry over to another area, so the calculator caps each area at its requirement.
