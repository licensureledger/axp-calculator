When can I retake a failed ARE division?

60 days after the failed attempt. One more limit applies: you can take the same division at most three times in any 12-month period.

Enter the date of each attempt at this division in the last 12 months (leave unused boxes empty).

Enter your most recent attempt date

How the two rules combine

Most candidates only meet the 60-day wait. If you have already taken the same division three times in the past 12 months, you also have to wait until the oldest of those attempts is more than 12 months old. The calculator above shows the later of the two dates.
