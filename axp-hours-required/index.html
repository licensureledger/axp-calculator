How many AXP hours do I need in each experience area?

3,740 hours in total, split across six experience areas. Each area has its own minimum, and hours above an area's minimum do not carry over to another area.

Experience areaHours required
Practice Management160
Project Management360
Programming & Analysis260
Project Planning & Design1,080
Project Development & Documentation1,520
Construction & Evaluation360
Total3,740

Why your total can look finished when it is not

If you have 2,000 hours in Project Development & Documentation, only 1,520 count toward the requirement. The other 480 do not fill a gap in Construction & Evaluation. Check each area, not just the total.
