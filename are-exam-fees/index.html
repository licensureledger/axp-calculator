How much does the ARE cost, and what do rescheduling and cancelling cost?

$263 per division, and a retake costs the same. Six divisions passed first time come to $1,578, plus the $105 NCARB Record application and $105 a year to keep it active.

Rescheduling, from October 1, 2026

Notice before the appointmentFee
31 days or more$0
15 to 30 days$75
7 to 14 days$150
Under 7 daysNot allowed

You can reschedule an appointment up to three times.

Cancelling

Cancelling costs $200 and keeps a seat credit for up to one year.

Budgeting for retakes

Every failed division adds $263 and at least 60 days. A firm that reimburses exam fees should budget for more than six sittings per candidate.
